I work on the Internationalization team at Google in Zürich. I'm a core
contributor to ICU4C (C++), ICU4J (Java), and ICU4X (Rust), and I also
contribute to Unicode CLDR (UTS #35). My work focuses on measurement unit
conversion and preferences, grammatical inflection, and number and currency
formatting. I've also mentored Google Summer of Code contributors and organized
Rust meetups hosted at Google.

Outside work, I love cooking, playing the guitar, hiking, and exploring new
cities.

Here I share blog posts, the slides and code from my talks, and notes from
meetups.
